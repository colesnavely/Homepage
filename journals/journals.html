My Website Is A Shifting House - August 8, 2026

Laura Schwulst is an American designer, artist, and writer. She was born in 1988 and was around 30 years old in 2018 when she wrote My Website Is A Shifting House. Schwulst uses different objects as metaphors for what web can be. She encourages web designers to explore different uses for websites and to make the web what you want it to be.


A Rant About Technology - August 8, 2026

Ursula K. Le Guin was an American author known for writing speculative fiction. She was born in 1929 and died in 2018. She was around 76 years old in 2005 when she wrote A Rant About Technology. Le Guin claims that current technology advancements overshadow everyday tools as technology. She thinks people should not overlook knives, wheels, clocks, and chairs as technology. Le Guin makes the argument that matches are seen as low-tech, but anyone who’s created fire without matches would see them as a high-tech invention. This article reminds us that websites are not simple and primitive technology. Despite technology making it easier to build websites, they are not simple. A lot of effort goes into website construction and hand-coding reveals the meticulous process of web design.


A Handmade Web - August 27, 2026

J.R. Carpenter is a British-Canadian artist and writer, she is known for working with print, performance, and digital media. She was born in 1972 and was around 42 years old in 2015 when she wrote A Handmade Web. When J.R. says “handmade web” she is referring to web pages coded by hand rather than by software. Handmade web is very personal, rich, and slow. Handmade web contradicts the motive of business and corporate web. Web can perform functions that traditional books are unable to do. In J.R.’s example, her Fishes & Flying Things book has an end, but her web page is continuous. When the handmade web emerged, print and digital media had a closer relationship. The growth of the internet and social media has inspired a sudden return to the version of the internet that was smaller and quieter. This discussion makes me think about how we are too reliant on the internet and the harmful impact of data centers trying to keep up with the growing internet. It is radical to hand-code and self-publish experimental web art.


Against an Increasingly User-Hostile Web - September 15, 2026

Parimal Satyal is a designer and writer who grew up in Nepal and lives in Berlin. He was around 28 years old when he wrote Against an Increasingly User-Hostile Web. The web used to be small and personal, now companies track the websites you visit and they steal your personal information. There are some options to minimize tracking such as Firefox, Ublock, etc. The web has evolved to fuel capitalism and make web-users into consumers.
