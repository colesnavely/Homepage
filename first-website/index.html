Hello World!

Perceptual illusions aren't tricks; they reveal shortcuts which the brain uses, and successfully most of the time.

They teach us about our visual perception, its strengths and its limitations.

Selection here emphasises beauty and interactive experiments; I also attempt explanations of the underlying visual mechanisms where possible.
